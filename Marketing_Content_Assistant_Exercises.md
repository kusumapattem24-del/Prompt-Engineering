# Marketing Strategy & Content Assistant Exercises

## Exercise 1 - Gather Brand Guidelines

**Task:** Collect brand guidelines, messaging framework, and (optionally) past campaign examples.

### Answer - Setup Steps
- Gather and upload the required knowledge files to the GPT.

## Exercise 2 - Build the Instruction Block

**Task:** Write instructions defining the GPT's content-generation capabilities, tone, and output structure.

### Answer - Setup Steps
- Define Role: Marketing Strategy & Content Assistant.
- Define output structure: Headline -> Body copy -> CTA.
- Save as instruction_block.md.

## Exercise 3 - Define Required Inputs and Brand Safety Guardrails

**Task:** Add rules requiring campaign goal, audience, platform, and tone before generating content, plus brand-safety constraints.

### Answer - Setup Steps
- Confirm campaign goal, target audience, platform, brand tone, and optional keywords before generating; ask clarifying questions if missing.
- No unverified claims, no prohibited words/phrases, must follow brand guidelines, avoid legal/compliance risk.

## Exercise 4 - Test Across Content Risk Scenarios

**Task:** Test a missing campaign goal, platform-specific request, over-promotional request, brand-unsafe wording, and request for alternatives.

### Answer - Setup Steps
- Run all five test scenarios.
- Record results in test_results.md.
